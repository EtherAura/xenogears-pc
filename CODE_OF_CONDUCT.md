# Code of conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests and Discussions in this repository, and
to everyone taking part, the maintainer included.

## Expected

- **Be civil.** Criticise the work, not the person.
- **Stay on topic.** Issues are for bugs, pull requests for changes, and
  Discussions for everything else.
- **Be fair to other Xenogears projects.** Do not belittle them here, and do
  not carry arguments into their spaces on this project's behalf.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, insults, slurs, harassment and threats.
- Posting anyone's private information.
- Pile-ons: accounts arriving together to repeat the same complaint.
- Spam and advertising.
- Links to disc images or any other copy of the game. Nothing from the game
  belongs here ([Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## How the project is made

How the project is made, from its tools and methods to what it works on next,
is the maintainer's decision. Disagreeing with it is fine; comments that only
argue it are off-topic and are hidden.

The project is [original work](README.md#original-work). Saying that something
here was copied is a serious claim, so make it with the evidence: open a
discussion naming the file and lines and where you believe they came from. It
will be checked, and anything copied will be removed.

## What happens

The maintainer may hide comments, lock or close threads, limit who can comment
for a while, and block accounts, usually in that order. Threats, slurs and
posted private information go straight to a block. To question a decision,
open one discussion and make your case once.

## Reporting

Point a problem out in the thread, or open a discussion. Abuse can also go to
GitHub: **Report content** in the comment's **…** menu.
